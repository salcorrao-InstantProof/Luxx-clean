LUXX v1.4.9 — EVIDENCE REPORTING
Built on v1.4.8. No new tabs. No new storage. No model calls. No recurring cost.

Everything added here is a pure derivation over data LUXX has always captured and never read.
Each one is only possible because the prescription is frozen at approval and the outcome ledger
is append-only. A tool without those two properties cannot make these claims honestly, which is
the point.

1. IS LUXX ACTUALLY RIGHT? (calibrationReport)
   LUXX grades its own advice. For every measured action it compares the prediction frozen at
   approval against what actually happened, using concordant/discordant pair counting.
   It reports one of: NOT ENOUGH DATA / NOT ENOUGH VARIATION / RANKING IS HOLDING UP /
   NO BETTER THAN CHANCE / RANKING IS BACKWARDS.
   RANKING IS BACKWARDS explicitly tells the creator to trust her own judgement over TODAY.

   Two design points found while building it:
   - It grades the PREDICTIVE part of the score only. The full candidate score includes the
     fatigue penalty, which is a rotation mechanism, not a forecast. Grading the combined number
     measured scheduling noise and produced a spurious negative correlation.
   - Thresholds are gated on the number of MEASURED RESULTS (>=20), not on pair count. N results
     produce ~N^2/2 pairs, so the pair count wildly overstates the evidence. The neutral band is
     +/-0.45 because simulated pure noise still reached tau=0.29.
   Validated against known ground truth: signal -> +0.85, pure noise -> +0.29 reported as
   chance, no-variation -> refuses to grade.

2. WHAT AN HOUR OF YOUR TIME IS WORTH (effortEconomics)
   creator_effort_minutes has been captured on every executed action since v1.2 and read by
   nothing except a small term in the score. LUXX now reports revenue per creator-hour overall
   and per route, and refuses to quote a rate below 6 posts.

3. WHEN YOU OVERRIDE LUXX, THAT IS DATA ABOUT LUXX (overrideSignal)
   Decline and change reasons have always been required and stored, and never read. If you
   override more than half the prescriptions for a destination, LUXX now says so in your words:
   "You have overridden 67% of LUXX prescriptions for Fansly. LUXX is probably wrong about that
   destination, not you."

4. RECEIPTS (proofPack) — new DOWNLOAD RECEIPTS button in RESULTS
   For every executed action: exactly what LUXX froze at approval, exactly what the creator did,
   exactly what was measured, every correction with its reason, and the attribution precision.
   Not a reconstruction. The frozen snapshot itself. Rows at PARTIAL or UNATTRIBUTED are marked
   as not causally creditable to LUXX.

5. SINCE BASELINE (sinceBaseline)
   The locked baseline existed and RESULTS never used it. LUXX now reports activity since the
   lock and states plainly how much of it can actually be credited: "No action yet carries EXACT
   attribution, so no revenue can be causally credited to LUXX."

WHY THIS IS THE DIFFERENTIATOR
Competing creator tools report what happened and imply they caused it. LUXX now reports what it
predicted, whether that prediction held, what the creator's time earned, where the creator keeps
disagreeing with it, and which revenue it is NOT allowed to claim. The moat is not the
recommender. It is that the ledger can prove or disprove the recommender.
