================================================================
FOR THE OPERATOR — READ THIS PART
================================================================
WHAT LUXX IS NOW, IN ONE LINE
  You already shot today's post. LUXX will tell you which one — and when it's just guessing.

WHAT YOU WILL SEE
  TODAY is now a short list of 3-7 real files from your own library. Each line says where to
  post it, why it is on the list in terms of YOUR results, and carries one of two stamps:

    CALL   6 or more measured results on that photo for that destination.
    GUESS  fewer than 6. LUXX is going on technical quality and rotation, not on evidence
           that the photo earns.

  On a new library every line will say GUESS, and the headline will say so first. That is
  correct. Every result you enter turns a guess into a call.

WHAT LUXX WILL NEVER DO
  Post for you. Message anyone. Run your page. Generate a photo or a body.
  It names files that already exist on your drive and tells you how sure it is.

WHAT YOU MUST NOT DO
  Deploy to the SAME Netlify site. Set LUXX_PASSCODE and LUXX_SESSION_SECRET first.

STILL NOT SAFE TO TRUST
  Auto-cropping (shape is checked, composition never is — crops stay manual).
  Branch Shoot concept matching (filenames and notes, not image content).
  Posting times, unless your timezone is set and you have 16+ measured results.

================================================================
ENGINEERING DETAIL
================================================================
LUXX v1.6.0 — TODAY'S PICK

TODAY was a single prescription. It is now the product: a short stamped list.

1. THE LIST
   buildTodayList() returns 3-7 lines, one per ASSET (deduplicated — a photo does not appear
   five times because it is eligible on five destinations, which is a warehouse tour, not a
   pick). Each line carries platform, placement, caption, why, and a stamp.

2. THE STAMP
   CALL_THRESHOLD = 6 measured results on that asset for that destination.
   Below it, GUESS, with the reason spelled out rather than implied. A stamp with no
   definition is decoration, so stamp_note states exactly what the stamp means on every line.
   The headline leads with the count of guesses instead of burying it.

3. THE REASON
   pickReason() is written in the creator's results and her posting history:
     "Your best performer on this route — 18 measured results; last posted 9 days ago."
     "LUXX has never measured this photo; never posted through LUXX."
   A test asserts the reason text contains no model language (model, algorithm, AI, score of,
   confidence interval). The reason is evidence, not flattery.

4. PICK ANY LINE, NOT JUST THE TOP ONE
   PRESCRIBE_PICK approves the exact line the creator chose and revalidates it against the
   live candidate set. A stale pick is refused with "reload TODAY and choose again" rather
   than silently prescribing something else.

5. COSTS NOTHING EXTRA
   The list is computed from the same scan as RECOMMEND and stored under the same generation,
   so the pick list never costs a second walk of the library. today-generation still proves
   5 repeated calls on a quiet library read 0 assets.

6. THE BUILD NOW REFUSES TO SHIP WITHOUT THE STAMP
   build-check.mjs fails the deploy if TODAY'S PICK, stampCall, stampGuess or approvePick
   vanish from the UI, or if the promise text and CALL_THRESHOLD vanish from the domain.
   Verified by deliberately removing a marker and confirming the build refuses.
   The stamp is the product. It must not be able to quietly regress into "suggested posts".
